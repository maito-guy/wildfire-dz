# Demo script (draft, target ≤ 3 min). Owner: Commerce, with the whole team rehearsing

Setup: laptop on the projector at `http://localhost:8000/dashboard/`, and 4 test phones armed at `http://<laptop-ip>:8000/alert/`.
Press **Reset demo** before every run.

1. **Problem (20 s).** The 2021 Kabylie fires killed 90 people. Satellite data arrives up to ~3 h late, and nobody tells a village how long it has.
2. **Replay (60 s).** Pick the Kabylie 2021 holdout and press Play. The predicted 1 h/2 h zones appear next to what really burned at the next pass (black dots). Point at the data age, e.g. "satellite pass 47 min ago".
3. **Minutes left (30 s).** The village list is sorted by minutes left. Show the counter: "Village X warned [measured] before the fire arrived; old method: [measured]". Use only numbers from the scoreboard, or say "illustrative".
4. **Judge clicks "Start a fire here" (40 s).** Zones are drawn and the phones sound within 30 s. Show the cancel button working.
5. **Honesty + roadmap (30 s).** Scoreboard vs baselines A/B; weather in training is reanalysis. Next: pilot wilaya 2027, Meteosat 15-min detection, real cell broadcast with Mobilis/Djezzy/Ooredoo.

Backup: a 2-min screen recording (Sat 10:00–14:00) in case the venue Wi-Fi fails. Map tiles need internet.
